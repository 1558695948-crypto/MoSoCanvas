# Evidence foundations

Use this file to audit the method. Evidence informs mechanisms and test design; it does not turn
contextual visual choices into universal laws.

## Story and shot construction

- Pixar in a Box's storytelling curriculum treats storyboarding and visual language as intentional
  construction rather than prompt decoration:
  https://www.khanacademy.org/humanities/grammar/storytelling
- American Cinematographer's Shot Craft material frames camera placement as a narrative decision
  with spatial consequences:
  https://theasc.com/articles/shot-craft-where-do-you-put-the-camera

MoSoCanvas operationalizes these practices as viewer position, information power, shot plans, and
composition proof. The specific schema fields are design controls, not scientific laws.

## Attention and gaze

- Tatler et al. review evidence that gaze behavior depends on both scene information and task:
  https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3134223/

This supports testing first read at the actual carrier and avoiding universal eye-path formulas. It
does not guarantee that a predicted fixation sequence will occur for every viewer.

## Color

- CIECAM16 formalizes color appearance under specified viewing conditions:
  https://www.cie.co.at/publications/cie-2016-colour-appearance-model-colour-management-systems-ciecam16

This supports separating measured color and viewing conditions from contextual interpretation.
MoSoCanvas's value/accent/light scripts are professional planning tools, not claims that hue causes
one universal emotion.

## Trend exposure and fixation

- CHI 2024 research on design fixation reports how examples can constrain later ideation:
  https://doi.org/10.1145/3613904.3642919
- Behance explains that featured work is curator-selected rather than a pure popularity ranking:
  https://help.behance.net/hc/en-us/articles/204483974-Guide-Featured-Projects
- Pinterest describes its trend forecasting as longitudinal behavioral analysis:
  https://business.pinterest.com/en-gb/blog/how-we-forecast-trends-pinterest-predicts/

These support native ideation before trend exposure and keeping curation, velocity, recurrence, and
saturation separate. They do not make any one platform representative of the whole design field.

## Aesthetic evaluation

- LAPIS presents a dataset for personalized image-aesthetic assessment, reinforcing that aesthetic
  judgment varies by viewer:
  https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2025W/CVEU/html/Maerten_LAPIS_A_novel_dataset_for_personalized_image_aesthetic_assessment_CVPRW_2025_paper.html
- Pick-a-Pic uses human preferences for text-to-image evaluation:
  https://papers.nips.cc/paper_files/paper/2023/hash/73aacd8b3b05b4b503d58310b523553c-Abstract-Conference.html

These support pairwise or rubric-based human judgment and separation of technical defects from
preference. They do not justify a universal automatic beauty score.

- Midjourney documents image-aesthetics selection, Explore curation, and pairwise rating for new
  versions:
  https://docs.midjourney.com/hc/en-us/articles/33390759197197-Complete-Tasks
- Midjourney's published magazine-selection criteria include clarity, originality, and composition:
  https://docs.midjourney.com/hc/en-us/articles/28012940139021-Midjourney-Magazine-Subscription-FAQ

These support treating Midjourney as a changing external benchmark evaluated through blinded
preferences and diagnostic dimensions. They do not justify copying its current look or declaring
superiority from an unmatched example.

## Validation integrity

- Agent Skills evaluation guidance recommends clean-context tests and observable success criteria:
  https://agentskills.io/skill-creation/evaluating-skills

This supports blind review packets, regression cases, and explicit release gates. A schema validator
can verify evidence structure but cannot verify that a visual claim is true; actual artifact
inspection and user acceptance remain separate.
