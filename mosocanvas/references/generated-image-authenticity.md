# Generated-image authenticity review

Review the artifact, not the prompt. “Looks AI” is not a diagnosis.

## Inspect by failure class

### Composition and attention

- centered spectacle without relational purpose;
- decorative foreground and empty middle distance;
- equal detail everywhere;
- accidental tangencies, mergers, or frame-edge amputations;
- gaze and gesture vectors that contradict the intended read.

### Physical and semantic continuity

- incompatible reflections, shadows, or light directions;
- object states that cannot coexist;
- repeated, fused, or incomplete anatomy;
- handles, straps, furniture, architecture, and interfaces that terminate or connect impossibly;
- local details that imply a different scene than the whole.

### Surface and process residue

- one microtexture field spread across unrelated materials;
- pseudo-lettering or meaningless interface marks;
- repeated decorative fragments;
- over-smoothed faces against over-detailed surroundings;
- bloom, dust, fog, chromatic fringing, or film grain applied uniformly as an “aesthetic” layer.

### Narrative cliché

- random eyes, portals, cables, smoke, screens, and particles used as generic intelligence;
- uncanny effect that disappears when decorative anomalies are removed;
- symbols that explain the concept instead of producing a viewer relation.

## Review scales

1. carrier thumbnail or intended viewing distance;
2. full frame;
3. 100–200% risky-region crops;
4. contact sheet for series continuity.

A defect can be harmless at use scale but still matter if it grows, repeats, breaks material logic,
or threatens downstream production.

## Independent review protocol

- Hide prompt, intended interpretation, model, and self-review during the first pass.
- Record first read, eye path, anomaly, and inferred narrative before seeing the spec.
- Reveal the spec and score fit separately from standalone craft.
- Identify at least one alternative explanation for a severity-2 or severity-3 claim.
- Require a named evidence region for every blocker.
- Treat subjective unresolved issues as `user-judgment`.

The reviewer must identify whether they are independent of the generating context. If not, the
review can guide iteration but cannot authorize release.
