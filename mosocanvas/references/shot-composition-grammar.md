# Shot and composition grammar

Use this before generating a zero-reference hero image or series.

## Build the shot from relations

Record these fields rather than prose such as “cinematic composition”:

- frame and intended crop;
- camera height, yaw, pitch, distance, and lens behavior;
- subject envelope as percentages of frame width and height;
- dominant mass, counter-mass, and negative-space owner;
- horizon or primary plane;
- gaze, gesture, motion, and architectural vectors;
- occlusion layers and depth order;
- highest contrast region and first fixation target;
- edge contacts, tangencies, and crop pressure;
- safe zones required by the carrier.

Percentages may be approximate during ideation; they become constraints after selection.

## Prove in monochrome

Make a 3–5 cm or 160–240 px value thumbnail. Use three values only:

- dark mass;
- middle field;
- light mass.

The thumbnail passes when:

- the first-read mass relation survives without texture;
- the intended subject does not depend on tiny details;
- negative space has a function;
- the eye path can be described in two or three moves;
- the crop does not create accidental tangencies;
- the carrier overlay does not destroy the hierarchy.

Color may strengthen a passed composition; it may not rescue a failed one.

## Use viewpoint as meaning

- Eye level invites equivalence.
- High angle can expose a system or reduce agency.
- Low angle can grant power but easily becomes generic monumentality.
- A camera hidden behind an obstruction creates witness or surveillance.
- Direct gaze creates an address; off-axis gaze redirects the viewer into the world.
- Excessive wide-angle distortion creates bodily and architectural anomalies; use it only when the
  distortion serves the proposition.

Treat these as contextual mechanisms, not universal emotional laws.

## Diagnose common generator defaults

- **Centered subject + symmetric aura:** instant icon, weak narrative time.
- **Foreground object + empty middle + spectacle background:** depth without relationship.
- **All edges equally detailed:** no attention control.
- **Every frame at medium distance:** series becomes a slideshow.
- **Incidental diagonals:** energy without intentional direction.
- **Decorative occlusion:** visual clutter that hides rather than reveals information.

Repair structure before adding detail or stronger styling.

## Series coverage

Across a series, deliberately distribute:

- establishing / relation / detail / reversal / aftermath;
- frontal / oblique / overhead or low / obstructed / absent-subject viewpoints;
- open / compressed / layered / void-dominant space;
- low / medium / high information density.

Repetition is allowed only when it establishes a rule that a later frame breaks.
